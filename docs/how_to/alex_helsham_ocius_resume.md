## Cover Letter

Dear Ocius,

I am interested in bringing my machine learning and data science experience to Ocius. I have spent eight years building and operationalising data-driven models, production pipelines, and monitoring workflows. The opportunity to help Ocius make better use of data from autonomous vessels is especially compelling: it combines an open-ended business question with the practical challenge of supporting systems operating for months at sea.

The vessel fouling and maintenance questions you are exploring are interesting because they call for more than a model in isolation. I would be keen to understand what vessel telemetry, operating conditions, inspection findings, maintenance records, and component histories are available, then work with the engineering and operations teams to determine which signals can support useful decisions. For hull fouling, that could mean investigating whether changes in performance or power consumption can indicate increasing drag, and validating any indicators against inspection and cleaning records. For component maintenance, it could mean assessing whether failure and service histories support reliable risk estimates, then translating those estimates into a practical maintenance-planning workflow. These are hypotheses to test with your team and data, not assumptions about what the data will show.

In my current role, I own production ML delivery and work across the lifecycle from problem framing and feature engineering to training, deployment, monitoring, and refresh. I have refactored shared MLOps workflows to support new model types without disrupting existing production models, and helped standardise data ingestion and feature transformation so teams can build on reusable, tested data. I have also taken models from batch scoring through to daily and real-time inference in collaboration with engineering teams. That experience would help me approach Ocius’s challenge pragmatically: establish what is measurable, prototype and validate useful signals, and build toward outputs that can support real operational decisions.

Alongside my professional work, I am building an autonomous person-following rover and have developed a custom computer-vision data and training pipeline for detecting 18-foot skiffs. These projects reflect my personal interest in robotics and sailing, while my production experience gives me a strong foundation for turning promising ideas into reliable workflows. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how a data science and machine learning approach could help Ocius make its vessel data more actionable.
